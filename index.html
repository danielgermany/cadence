How it works

01

Connect when you're ready

Availability shows who's free right now. If no one is, leave a message with why you're reaching out and when to reconnect — you'll hear back, or get an automatic refund after 24 hours.

02

Talk in any language

The chat box auto-translates as you type, so language is never the barrier.

03

Pause without losing time

Put a conversation on hold and pick it up later with the same Friend or a different one — the clock waits with you.

04

Leave whenever you want

End a conversation any time. The transcript is yours alone; a Friend can only share it to suggest a referral, and only with your OK.

Why Cadence

Not therapy. Not your group chat.

Hiring a coach or therapist costs more than most people can spend on a hard week. Cadence gives you somewhere to talk it out first — freely, with no strings attached — for as long or as little as you need. Want more structure? Moving to a professional coaching session is one tap away.

Open to adults of any age, and to teens with signed parental consent.

Pricing

Pay for what you use

Chat & SMS

$1 per message you send. Texting is unlimited — only messages you send count against the clock.

By the hour

$25 for one full hour block, phone or chat. Your Friend decides whether to continue or wrap up.

Getting started

A $5 hold on your card, digital wallet, or bank account. Nothing else is charged until you send.

Going professional

Package pricing is available if you want a structured coaching session instead of open chat.
